Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2, 2026

1. Information We Collect

LO-F! AI collects minimal data necessary to provide music bot services:

  • User IDs: Discord user IDs for greeting new users and tracking usage
  • Server IDs: Discord server IDs to manage bot settings per server
  • Command Usage: Commands used to improve bot functionality
  • Music Preferences: Saved playlists and music history (optional)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide music playback services
  • Remember user preferences and settings
  • Send welcome messages to new users
  • Improve bot performance and features
  • Maintain 24/7 music stations

3. Data Storage

All data is stored securely on our servers. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

  • User data is stored in encrypted JSON files
  • No personal information beyond Discord IDs is collected
  • Data is retained only as long as necessary for bot functionality

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties. Your information is used solely for bot operation.

5. Third-Party Services

LO-F! AI uses the following third-party services:

  • Discord API: For bot functionality
  • Lavalink: For music streaming
  • YouTube/Spotify: For music search and playback

These services have their own privacy policies.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request deletion of your data (contact us on Discord)
  • Opt-out of data collection by removing the bot
  • Access your stored data upon request

7. Children's Privacy

LO-F! AI complies with Discord's Terms of Service. Users must be 13+ years old to use Discord and our bot.

8. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Continued use of the bot constitutes acceptance of changes.
